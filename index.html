From Nairobi to your kitchen

Kilima Roasters started in a small Nairobi garage with one roaster and a simple idea: Kenyan coffee should taste as good at home as it does at the farm.

We buy directly from cooperative growers, roast in modest batches, and print the roast date on every bag so you always know how fresh it is.

Our mission is simple: make exceptional coffee accessible, transparent, and enjoyable for everyday brewing.

Whether you’re brewing a quick filter at home or hosting friends for a weekend tasting, we aim to make each cup feel special.
